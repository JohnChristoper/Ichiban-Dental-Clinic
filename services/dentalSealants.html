Dental Sealants

What Are Dental Sealants?

Dental Sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth (molars and premolars). These areas are more prone to cavities due to deep grooves where food and bacteria can easily accumulate. Sealants act as a barrier, protecting the enamel from plaque and acids that can cause tooth decay.

What Happens During the Procedure??

The application of dental sealants is quick, simple, and painless. Your dentist will first clean and dry the tooth, then apply a special solution to prepare the surface. The sealant material is then carefully painted onto the tooth and hardened using a curing light. Once set, it forms a protective shield over the grooves of the tooth.

Why Are Dental Sealants Important?

Even with proper brushing, it can be difficult to clean the deep grooves of molars thoroughly. Sealants provide extra protection against cavities, especially in these hard-to-reach areas.

This treatment helps:

  • Prevent tooth decay on chewing surfaces
  • Protect vulnerable areas of the teeth
  • Reduce the risk of cavities in children and adults
  • Support long-term oral health

Who Should Get Dental Sealants?

Dental sealants are commonly recommended for children and teenagers as soon as their permanent molars come in. However, adults without cavities in their molars can also benefit from this preventive treatment.

How Long Do Sealants Last?

Sealants can last for several years with proper care. Your dentist will check them during regular visits and reapply them if necessary to maintain protection.

Extra Protection for a Healthier Smile

Dental Sealants are a simple and effective way to prevent cavities and protect your teeth. Combined with good oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, they help keep your smile strong and healthy.
