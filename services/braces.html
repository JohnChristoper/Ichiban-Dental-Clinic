Braces

What Are Braces?

Braces are orthodontic appliances used to correct misaligned teeth, bite issues, and jaw irregularities. They work by applying gentle, continuous pressure to gradually move teeth into their proper position over time. This treatment not only improves the appearance of your smile but also enhances overall oral function.

What Happens During the Treatment?

The process begins with a thorough assessment of your teeth and bite. Your dentist or orthodontist will then attach brackets to your teeth and connect them using wires and elastic bands. Regular adjustments are scheduled to gradually shift your teeth into alignment. The duration of treatment varies depending on the complexity of your case.

Why Choose Braces?

Braces are a reliable and effective solution for correcting a wide range of orthodontic problems.

This treatment helps:

  • Straighten crooked or crowded teeth
  • Correct bite issues (overbite, underbite, crossbite)
  • Improve oral hygiene by making teeth easier to clean
  • Enhance overall smile appearance and confidence

Are Braces Comfortable?

You may experience mild discomfort or pressure, especially after adjustments, but this is normal and temporary. Modern braces are designed to be more comfortable and efficient than ever before. Your dentist will guide you on how to manage any discomfort during treatment.

How Long Does the Procedure Take?

Treatment time typically ranges from several months to a few years, depending on the severity of the alignment issues and your individual response to treatment.

Achieve a Straighter, Healthier Smile

Braces provide a proven way to align your teeth and improve your bite. With consistent care and regular visits, you can achieve a confident, well-aligned smile that lasts a lifetime.
