Root Canal Treatment

What Is a Root Canal Treatment?

Root Canal Treatment is a dental procedure used to treat infection or damage within the inner part of the tooth, known as the pulp. This treatment helps save the natural tooth by removing the infected tissue and sealing it to prevent further problems. It is often recommended when a tooth becomes severely decayed, infected, or painful.

What Happens During the Procedure?

During the procedure, your dentist will carefully remove the infected or damaged pulp from inside the tooth. The inner canals are then cleaned, disinfected, and shaped. Once cleaned, the canals are filled and sealed with a special material. In many cases, a dental crown is placed afterward to restore the tooth’s strength and function.

Why Is Root Canal Treatment Important?

Ignoring an infected tooth can lead to more serious complications, including abscess formation or tooth loss. Root canal treatment helps eliminate infection while preserving your natural tooth.

This treatment helps:

  • Relieve pain and discomfort
  • Remove infection from the tooth
  • Prevent the spread of bacteria
  • Save the natural tooth from extraction

Is the Procedure Painful?

Root canal treatment is generally not painful, as it is performed under local anesthesia. Most patients feel relief from pain after the procedure rather than discomfort. Mild sensitivity may occur afterward, but it usually subsides within a few days.

How Long Does the Process Take?

The treatment may be completed in one or more visits, depending on the severity of the infection and the condition of the tooth.

Save Your Natural Tooth

Root Canal Treatment is an effective way to treat infection while preserving your natural smile. With proper care and follow-up, the treated tooth can last for many years.
