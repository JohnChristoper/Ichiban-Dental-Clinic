Dental Fillings

What Are Dental Fillings?

Dental Fillings are a restorative treatment used to repair teeth that have been damaged by cavities or minor fractures. The procedure involves removing the decayed portion of the tooth and filling the space with a durable, tooth-colored material to restore its shape and function. This treatment helps prevent further decay while maintaining the natural appearance of your teeth

What Happens During the Procedure?

During the procedure, your dentist will carefully remove the decayed or damaged area of the tooth. The cleaned space is then filled with a composite or other suitable material. Once placed, the filling is shaped and polished to match your natural tooth structure, ensuring a comfortable bite and a seamless look.

Why Are Dental Fillings Important?

Untreated cavities can worsen over time and lead to more serious dental problems. Dental fillings help restore the integrity of the tooth and protect it from further damage.

This treatment helps:

  • Stop the progression of tooth decay
  • Restore tooth strength and function
  • Prevent further damage or infection
  • Maintain a natural-looking smile

Are Dental Fillings Durable?

Modern dental fillings, especially tooth-colored composites, are durable and designed to withstand everyday chewing forces. With proper care and regular dental visits, they can last for many years.

How Long Does the Procedure Take?

Dental fillings are usually completed in a single visit, depending on the number of teeth being treated and the extent of the decay.

Restore and Protect Your Teeth

Dental Fillings provide a simple and effective way to repair damaged teeth and maintain your oral health. Early treatment helps preserve your natural teeth and prevents more complex procedures in the future.
