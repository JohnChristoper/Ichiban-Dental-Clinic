Removable Partial Dentures

What Are Removable Partial Dentures?

Removable Partial Dentures are dental appliances used to replace one or more missing teeth while preserving the remaining natural teeth. They consist of replacement teeth attached to a gum-colored base, often supported by a lightweight metal or flexible framework. They are designed to restore both function and appearance while fitting comfortably in your mouth

What Happens During the Procedure?

The process begins with a dental examination and impressions of your teeth and gums. These are used to create a custom-fit partial denture that matches your natural teeth. Once fabricated, the denture is fitted and adjusted to ensure comfort, proper alignment, and a secure fit. Your dentist will also provide guidance on how to wear and care for your appliance.

Why Choose Removable Partial Dentures?

Partial dentures are a practical solution for replacing missing teeth without the need for more complex procedures.

This treatment helps:

  • Restore chewing and speaking ability
  • Prevent remaining teeth from shifting
  • Improve overall smile appearance
  • Provide a removable and convenient option

Are Partial Dentures Comfortable?

Removable partial dentures are designed for comfort, but it may take time to get used to wearing them. Minor adjustments may be needed to ensure a proper fit and avoid discomfort. With regular use and proper care, they can become a natural part of your daily routine.

How Long Does the Process Take?

The process typically takes a few weeks, depending on the number of fittings and adjustments needed to achieve the best fit and function.

Restore Function and Balance to Your Smile

Removable Partial Dentures offer an effective and affordable way to replace missing teeth while maintaining your natural ones. They help restore both function and confidence, allowing you to smile with ease.
