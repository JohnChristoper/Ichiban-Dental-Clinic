Dental Crowns

What Are Dental Crowns?

Dental Crowns are custom-made caps placed over a damaged or weakened tooth to restore its shape, size, strength, and appearance. They fully cover the visible portion of the tooth, providing protection while improving its overall function.Crowns are commonly used when a tooth is too damaged for a filling but can still be preserved

What Happens During the Procedure?

The procedure usually requires two visits. During the first appointment, your dentist will prepare the tooth by removing a portion of its outer structure to make space for the crown. An impression is then taken and sent to a dental laboratory for fabrication. A temporary crown may be placed while waiting. On your next visit, the custom crown is fitted, adjusted, and permanently bonded for a secure and natural-looking result.

Why Are Dental Crowns Important?

Dental crowns are an effective solution for restoring both the strength and appearance of damaged teeth. They are designed to function like natural teeth while providing long-term protection.

This treatment helps:

  • Restore severely damaged or decayed teeth
  • Protect weak teeth from further fracture
  • Improve tooth shape, size, and appearance
  • Strengthen teeth after root canal treatment

Are Dental Crowns Durable?

Dental crowns are made from strong materials such as porcelain, ceramic, or metal, making them highly durable. With proper care, they can last for many years while maintaining their function and appearance.

How Long Does the Procedure Take?

The process typically takes two visits, allowing time for precise preparation and customization of the crown to ensure a proper fit.

Restore Strength and Confidence

Dental Crowns offer a reliable way to protect and restore damaged teeth while enhancing your smile. With their durability and natural look, they are a trusted solution for long-term oral health.
